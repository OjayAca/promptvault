Replacement Rules

How to Customize a Prompt

Every prompt uses [BRACKETS] as placeholders. Here's how to customize them for premium results.

Substitute this label with your precise assignment, theme, or research domain.

Example: Replace [TOPIC] with "Wastewater treatment solutions in suburban Manila communities"

Tell the AI the expected writing complexity, lexical variety, and tone suitable for the classroom.

Example: Replace [GRADE LEVEL] with "Grade 11 Senior High" or "College Freshman"

This allows the copy to adapt to the specific desires, colloquial styles, and struggles of your demographic.

Example: Replace [TARGET AUDIENCE] with "Working mothers in urban centers looking for quick meals"

Adjusts the emotional, structural, and professional balance of sentences to fit social branding.

Example: Replace [TONE] with "conversational Taglish with high energy" or "highly academic, formal and logical"

Insert your actual brand name, online shop name, startup, or freelance agency description here.

Example: Replace [BUSINESS NAME] with "Aling Nena's Savory Sizzlers"
